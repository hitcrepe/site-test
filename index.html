What is Displayd?

Displayd lets you create your own physical shelf filled with any media you can imagine and display them in a digital format.

With Displayd, you can:

  • Feature any media you like

  • Share with a like-minded community

  • Upload and display media not yet in our database

  • Customize and accessorize your media shelf!
